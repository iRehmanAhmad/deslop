THE GILDED PALM

Hotel · Ocean Drive · Est. 1932

Cocktails at six · Orchestra at eight · Swimming until midnight

The Gilded Palm has stood on Ocean Drive since the winter the neon arrived. Ninety-four rooms, one elevator that a man named Rosario still operates by hand, and a lobby ceiling painted with the constellations as they appeared on the night we opened, 14 December 1932.

— THE HOUSE RULES —

Dinner jackets are not required; they are merely admired. The pool is closed during thunderstorms and operatic arguments. Telegrams may be sent from the front desk until ten. Checkout is at noon, but the veranda keeps its own hours, and so, in our experience, do our guests.
