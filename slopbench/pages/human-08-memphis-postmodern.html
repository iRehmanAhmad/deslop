We are a graphic studio founded the year the Memphis Group broke up, by designers who never got over it. Laminates, squiggles, terrazzo, clashing pastels: if a layout makes a Swiss typographer sit down quietly, we frame it.

Squiggle Systems

Identity for a Rotterdam noodle chain. The logo is a squiggle. The squiggle is trademarked. Lawyers cried; sales rose.

Terrazzo Everything

A furniture catalogue printed to look like a kitchen worktop. Chairs sold separately, dignity not included.

Anti-Grid Posters

Fourteen posters for the city archive, each ignoring the grid in a different historical style, 1981 to 1994.

Manifesto (short version)

1. A triangle is a fine place to put a paragraph. 2. Pastel and neon are a colour story, actually. 3. If the client asks for "timeless", show them a lava lamp. 4. Fun is a grid too, it is just diagonal.
