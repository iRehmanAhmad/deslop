From the winter catalogue

Shelf IV · No. 12

Horace, Odes — Aldine Press, Venice, 1501

The small octavo that taught Europe a pocket book could hold a classic. Our copy carries the marginalia of three owners, the last of whom argued with the second in the margins of Book III. £14,500.

Shelf IV · No. 31

A commonplace book, kept by a Shropshire rector, 1719–1760

Four hundred pages of sermons he disagreed with, two cures for warts, and one recipe for plum pudding that he copied out four times, each time slightly more wrong. £3,200.

Shelf IX · No. 3

Atlas of the Mughal Empire, folio, Amsterdam, c. 1680

Hand-coloured maps, sea monsters in the Indian Ocean, and a Delhi drawn almost entirely from rumour. £22,000, or near offers from institutions that will let schoolchildren see it.

Visiting

The Athenaeum keeps shop hours the way its founder did: Tuesday to Friday, ten until four, and never when it rains hard enough to threaten the stock. Write first, and we shall put the kettle on and the good catalogue on the counter.
