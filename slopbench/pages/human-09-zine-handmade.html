CRUST #12

A zine about gigs in Bradford, written on the bus, laid out on the library photocopier that only jams on page 9.

FIRST: sorry about issue #11 being mostly a review of a gig that got cancelled. The band has since un-cancelled themselves, so the review stands, just early.

GIG OF THE MONTH: The Sump Pumps at the 1 in 12 Club, Friday. Three chords, one of them wrong, all of them loud. The support act was the singer's dad doing Springsteen covers on a ukulele and honestly? He stole the night. £4 in, no booking fee, bring earplugs if you're over 25 or value your Tuesday.

[photo: the Sump Pumps, blurred, everyone's mid-jump, photocopied twice so it looks like weather]

VENUE NEWS: The Exchange has fixed the toilet door. Repeat: the toilet door works now. In other news, the new place on Kirkgate does gigs in the basement and the ceiling is 6 foot 1, so tall bassists beware.

LETTER OF THE MONTH (from Gaz, Shipley): "Dear CRUST, your review said our demo sounds like it was recorded in a bin. It was. Love, Gaz." We stand corrected and also correct.

NEXT ISSUE: the great Bradford chip shop power rankings, definitive edition, and an interview with a band whose name we can't print because of the library's content filter.
