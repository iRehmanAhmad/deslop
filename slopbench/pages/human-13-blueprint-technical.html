NOTES

1. Chainstay lengthened 10 mm over Rev B to clear a 38 mm tyre with mudguards. This is the change testers asked for after the Pennine ride; do not revert it to make the drawing prettier.

2. Down tube is 31.8 mm, single-butted. The fancy ovalised tube from the catalogue saves 40 grams and costs us two hours of mitring. It is not in this drawing.

3. Braze-ons: three bottle mounts, rack eyes front and rear, dynamo wire guide on the right fork blade. Cable stops brazed, never clamped, on any frame leaving this workshop.

TOLERANCES

Frame alignment: ±0.5 mm over the wheelbase. Dropout faces parallel within 0.2 mm. If a frame leaves tolerance, it becomes the workshop's winter bike and the customer gets the next one off the jig. This has happened twice in eleven years; both frames are still being ridden.
