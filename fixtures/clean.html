The Sunday Repair Letter

Every Sunday morning: one broken household thing, diagnosed in plain language, with the exact part number you need and what it should cost.

What readers fixed last month

The washing machine that walked

Transit bolts left in after delivery. Four bolts, ten minutes, no parts to buy.

A fridge door that would not seal

The gasket had hardened. Warm water and a hairdryer brought back most doors; a replacement gasket is part DG9-441 and costs about 18 dollars.

Written by a person, in a shed, every week since 2021.
