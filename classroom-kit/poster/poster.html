A field guide for sharp eyes

SPOT THE SLOP!

Ten signs a website was made by AI — AI website builders make pages that all look the same. Train your eyes on these ten tells, and you will spot a copy-paste website anywhere.

1

The Purple–Blue Fade

The page top melts from purple into blue. Real designers pick colours on purpose — AI picks this one every time.

2

One Font for Everything

AaAaAaAa

Headings, text, buttons — all in the same plain font. A page with no font choices is a page nobody designed.

3

Emoji Doing the Icons' Job

🚀 ⭐ ✅ 🔥

Rockets, stars and ticks instead of real icons. Emoji change on every phone — real icons are drawn for the job.

4

Lorem Ipsum Left Behind

Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit, sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore…

Fake Latin words still sitting on the finished page. It means nobody even read the page before publishing it.

5

Everything in the Middle

Every line is centred; nothing lines up at the edges. Centring everything means nobody chose a layout.

6

The Shiny Badge Pill

★ NEW · AI-POWERED

A little lozenge floats over the headline shouting “NEW!” It's decoration dressed up as news.

7

Three Same-Same Cards

Three boxes in a row: same shape, same words. If they could swap places unnoticed, they say nothing.

8

“Get Started” Buttons

Get StartedLearn More

Get started doing what? A good button names the job: “Download the worksheet”, “Book a table”.

9

The Fancy Café Look

Maison Digitale — studio

Cream background, posh serif letters, soft sage green. The new “classy” costume AI wears to look expensive.

10

Glassy Blur Cards

frosted panel

Frosted see-through panels float over coloured blobs. Pretty fog, zero information — content should come first.
